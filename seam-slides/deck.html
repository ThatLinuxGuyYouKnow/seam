The problem

Internet fails. Money shouldn't.

Expensive data and spotty 3G/4G make regular Lightning wallets fail mid-payment, and the receiver must be online right then.

1

Data is expensive

Every retry, every failed payment burns scarce airtime.

2

3G drops mid-payment

A wallet that loses signal halfway leaves your sats stuck.

3

Receiver must be online

Signal, a smartphone and a funded wallet right now.

Why today's wallets break here

Both ends must be online. And stay online.

1

Always-on assumption

Retries burn data, and a dropped connection mid-payment strands the sats.

2

Receiver online now

They need a smartphone, a wallet and signal at this exact moment.

3

Tiny amounts

Fees and friction eat transport fares and market payments.

Seam only needs the sender online, briefly.

Seam03 / 10
The solution

Swap sats for bytes. Send the bytes.

1

Mint

Lightning → Cashu. You get a bearer token just a few hundred bytes long.

2

Send

Hand it over Nostr DM (NIP-17), SMS, WhatsApp, or an offline QR, anywhere text goes.

3

Claim

The recipient imports it and melts back to Lightning when they reconnect.

The recipient needs zero internet until they claim.

How to use · step 1

Mint: top up in one tap

  1. 1Open Mint, enter an amount, try 100 sats, and add a memo.
  2. 2Tap “Mint 100 sats (demo)” and your balance updates instantly.
  3. 3Demo OFF? You get a real Lightning invoice + QR on testnet.
Demo mode: instant · offline · zero sats
Seam06 / 10
How to use · step 2

Send: seal it, pick a channel

  1. 1Enter an amount and a memo, then “seal the token”.
  2. 2Share it: Nostr DM, show the QR, or copy the text into SMS / WhatsApp.
  3. 3No signal? Failed DMs wait in the outbox and retry on reconnect.
Token ≈ 200–400 bytes. It's just text
Seam07 / 10
How to use · step 3

Receive: claim when you reconnect

  1. 1Pull the Nostr inbox, paste the SMS text, or scan the QR, even offline.
  2. 2Tap Claim and the balance appears on their phone.
  3. 3Melt back to Lightning whenever they need it live.
Zero internet needed until claim time
